# Current settlement system

Order acceptance and regulated settlement are separately owned/deployed services with separate databases; neither team can write the other's tables. Existing durable message service can retain seven days. Settlement must survive a 24-hour downstream outage. Committed acceptance must never lose settlement intent. Delivery may duplicate and an acknowledgment may be lost after commit. Per-order transitions must not be applied out of order. Financial effects must not duplicate. Existing APIs have ten external consumers with staggered releases. Operators can quarantine and replay failed work. Propose design and recovery tests, not implementation. Legal compensation rules for charged-but-unfulfilled orders remain unresolved; identify the decision without inventing it.
