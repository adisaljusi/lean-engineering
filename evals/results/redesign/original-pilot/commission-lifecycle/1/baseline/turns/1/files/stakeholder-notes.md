# Commission change request

The existing treaty portal serves contract reviewers and approvers at reinsurance
companies. A treaty can have multiple sections with different commission rates.
Users already sign in and access records only for their own organization.
Original imported documents and previous published contract values are retained.
This request concerns commission changes when an addendum is uploaded.

## Existing domain vocabulary

- Treaty: the contract identified by the existing treaty ID.
- Section: an identified part of that treaty, such as property or casualty.
- Commission: a percentage attached to a section; not a monetary payment.
- Addendum: a document associated with a treaty; it may be a draft or signed.
- Published value: the value currently shown as approved in the portal.

## Stakeholder messages, not approved policy

Operations: "When a new addendum arrives, update the commission so reviewers do
not have to retype everything. The most recently uploaded document should win."

Underwriting: "Drafts are often uploaded after signed documents. Never let an
unsigned draft change a published value. Sometimes a signed change applies to
only one section or is effective before the upload date."

Product: "Make this automatic and fast. Keep the previous values available for
audit. We have not agreed whether automatic means a suggested change or a
published change. No performance or quality target has been approved."

No decision has been recorded about conflicting signed documents, precedence,
retroactive changes, publication approval or what a repeated upload should do.
The statements above are the current evidence, not an approval to choose a rule.
